🧪 Metal Salts

Welcome to Flame Test Lab!

Click on any metal salt to observe the characteristic flame color produced when heated.

Scientific Principle: When metal salts are heated, electrons in metal ions absorb energy and jump to higher energy levels. When they return to ground state, they emit light of specific wavelengths, creating characteristic colors.
